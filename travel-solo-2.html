Stay safe

Safety for Solo Travelers

Solo travel is safe for millions every year. These habits keep it that way and let you relax into the trip.

Personal safety
Stay aware, stick to well-lit areas at night, and trust your instincts — leave anything that feels off.
Emergency planning
Save local emergency numbers and your embassy, and keep a backup of key documents.
Respect local customs
Learn the norms and dress codes — blending in keeps you safer and more welcome.
Digital security
Be careful on public Wi-Fi, use a VPN for sensitive logins, and keep devices locked.
Stay connected
Keep your phone charged, share your itinerary with someone you trust, and check in regularly.
Be discreet
There's no need to advertise that you're travelling alone or exactly where you're staying.

For a full breakdown of personal safety, health, and digital security, see the Safe Travel Tips guide.
