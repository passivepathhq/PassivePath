Packing by Trip Type

Different trips need different bags. Pick the type that matches your plans and pack around it.

Weekend Getaway
Two or three days. One small bag, a few outfits, the essentials only.
International Travel
Documents, adapters, and layers. Plan for changing climates and longer days.
Beach Vacation
Light, breathable clothing, swimwear, sun protection, and sandals.
Mountain Trip
Warm layers, sturdy footwear, and weatherproof outerwear.
Business Travel
Wrinkle-resistant formalwear, tech, and a tidy carry-on that moves fast.
Adventure Travel
Durable, quick-dry gear, a solid pack, and only what you can carry.

Pack like a pro

Packing Tips

Small habits that save space, keep you organised, and avoid the usual mistakes.

01
Roll, don't fold
Rolling soft clothes saves space and reduces creases compared to folding.
02
Use packing cubes
Group items by category so you find things fast and repack in seconds.
03
Wear your bulkiest items
Put on your heaviest shoes and jacket for travel to free up bag space.
04
Keep valuables in your carry-on
Documents, tech, and anything irreplaceable never go in checked luggage.
05
Leave room to spare
Pack to about three-quarters full so you have space for anything you pick up.
06
Pack from a list
Working from a checklist is the simplest way to never forget the essentials.
