Before You Buy
Beginner Tips
Start Small
You don't need a complete home gym on day one. A mat and a resistance band set is genuinely enough to begin building strength and consistency — add equipment only when you've outgrown what you have.
Stay Consistent
Simple routines produce better long-term results than complicated ones done inconsistently. Three sessions a week with basic equipment beats seven sessions of a perfectly designed programme you abandon after two weeks.
Choose Comfort
Buy equipment you'll actually enjoy using. The best resistance bands are the ones you reach for. Prioritise feel and usability over specification — cheap uncomfortable gear is the equipment that ends up unused.
Upgrade Slowly
Add more equipment as your routine grows, not before. Buying equipment in advance of needing it is one of the most reliable ways to spend money on things that never get used. Earn each upgrade.
